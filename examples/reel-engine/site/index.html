Before you begin.

Is this an automatic viral-reel machine?

No. It is an agent-guided production workflow. You supply intent, footage and voice examples; research and edits still need judgment. No views, retention or revenue results are guaranteed.

What do I need to run it?

Codex with plugin support, Git, Python 3.11, Node.js and FFmpeg/FFprobe. Some stages need your own authenticated providers. The setup guide covers the dependencies and first-run checks.

Will it sound like me?

Bring your own scripts and voice samples. The workflow calibrates drafts to those examples and preserves your recorded voice. Private voice corpora are not bundled.

Does it publish or send messages for me?

It prepares giveaway resources, Skool post packages and ManyChat handoff packets. Publication and messaging are separate actions. Account connections are not included with installation.

Is the plugin open source?

The source is public, with rights retained unless separately licensed. Check the repository license for permitted use. Provider subscriptions and media rights are separate.
