# Reel Engine launch site

Creative direction delegated by Samin. Product claims checked against the current public plugin README and showcase, not inferred from historical memory.

## Audience and promise
AI creators who have ideas and footage but lose continuity across research, scripting, proof, editing and giveaway fulfillment. Promise: a repeatable, inspectable workflow from story to reel to resource. No promised view counts, revenue, retention lift or autonomous publishing.

## Buyer journey and feeling curve
1. Recognition: scattered creative work becomes one physical 3D engine. Headline: Your next reel needs a system.
2. Relief: scattered inputs arrange into a coherent pipeline. The user can explore Research, Script, Edit, Review and Deliver.
3. Trust: watch the actual 49-second exported example, with truthful production details and music attribution.
4. Ownership: see which bottleneck the pipeline solves and the exact first prompt to use.
5. Confidence: clear requirements, source-access distinction and provider boundaries.
6. Action: optional email signup for product updates, immediately available setup guide, and direct repository access. Payment only when offer and checkout details are supplied.

## Grammar and signature
Split-stage product narrative: a real WebGL assembly of film plates and a reel screen on the right, semantic copy on the left. The signature move is an exploded reel stack that compresses into one coherent unit as the visitor explores pipeline stages. Native page navigation and an explicit stage selector support purposeful browsing. No camera-flight simulation, fake live software dashboard or generated customer proof.

Peak: the abstract film assembly resolves into a real rendered reel. A visitor should say: It is the site where all the pieces of a reel come together, then you see the actual result.

## Brand
Samin Reel Engine; by Samin Yasar. Existing product identity has pale purple, warm white and deep ink. Use the pale purple as a solid accent, avoid neon gradients. Editorial condensed headline and neutral body typography. Technical provider names only when explaining requirements or source provenance.

## Layers and mobile
Rear orbital guides; middle exploded film plates; foreground phone with real reel poster. Separate depth, shared central axis. Scroll and stage selection change the assembly. Mobile places the 3D object below a compact headline; no pinned empty space. Reduced motion renders one settled scene. Missing WebGL uses the real poster.
