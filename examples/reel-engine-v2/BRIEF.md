# Reel Engine: Proof to Pixels

Rebuild requested after the first site's visual and motion direction failed the brief. Creative direction delegated.

Concept: a filmstrip made from actual rendered frames travels from scattered fragments to an ordered reel. The product is a connected process, so the scroll performs that connection. Warm paper, near-black ink, one vivid orange accent and condensed editorial type replace the earlier purple software-page styling. This is a new proposed campaign direction, not a claimed pre-existing brand kit.

Grammar: rhythmic product cutlist. One sustained 3D opening; a quiet typographic argument; a sideways stage procession; a video-scrub payoff; an actionable ending. Native scroll, no wheel interception.

Engine use: actual Astra Designer mount, pin progress and cue windows, pan, scrub with seek encoding, and flow entry. Bespoke Three.js geometry reads the engine's published --sc-p. No fabricated product UI.

Feeling curve: curiosity (physical reel ribbon); relief (pieces align); recognition (handoff problem named); clarity (outputs of each stage); confidence (actual video becomes playable); action (signup and install).

Opening / midpoint / exit: loose 3D film frames rotate around a common axis; frames align into a clean film ribbon; frames settle toward one finished vertical output. Header and CTA stay usable. Mobile changes camera distance, headline size, and geometry scale. Reduced motion uses a settled composition and exposes all story copy without pinned travel.

Evidence: actual v0.3.0 showcase frames and video, sourced from the user's public repository. No new claim about performance or subscribers. Lead signup uses existing private collection on the established live site. Payments remain unconfigured, as no price or checkout was supplied.
