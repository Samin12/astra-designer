The signup form collects your email address, your selected workflow interest and consent to receive Samin Reel Engine product updates. Records are stored in Samin's private site database on here.now. Other site visitors cannot read the signup list.

Removal requests

Use the here.now dashboard to contact the hosting provider about platform data, or contact Samin through his public profile to request removal from the product list. Do not post your email address in a public GitHub issue.

Site operation

The host processes requests to serve the site and protect the signup endpoint from abuse. The site has no third-party advertising pixels. The video loads only when requested. No payment details are collected here.
